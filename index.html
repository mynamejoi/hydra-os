Hydra-OS

Your day, your goals and what you're curious about, on pages that live inside Claude.

Home, Goals and Research are free. Wellbeing and more pages are on Home's Shop page.

Open the Claude app on your Mac. Start a new chat in the Code tab. Paste this line and press Return.

Hydra-OS puts your pages in a Hydra-OS folder on your Mac, with its own copy of Python. Two helpers start when you log in, to show your pages and keep them fresh, and a short morning review uses a little of your Claude plan each day, and a bit more on Mondays, when Research looks into your goals. Nothing about you is sent anywhere. Turn any of it off, or remove Hydra-OS, in Help.

Install Hydra-OS from https://github.com/mynamejoi/hydra-os, with its helpers and morning review on.

Claude says what it sets up, then sets it up, in the same reply. It takes a few minutes. Needs a Mac with macOS 13 or later and a Claude plan (Pro or higher).

You need nothing but the Claude app. Hydra-OS brings its own copy of Python, checked before it's used.

Your records stay as files on your Mac. There's no server of ours.
