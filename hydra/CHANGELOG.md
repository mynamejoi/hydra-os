# Hydra-OS

Each version's notes, newest first. Home's What's new shows a version's first three lines once, after an update.

## 1.0.0

- **Set up in your own chat.** Hydra-OS installs and sets itself up inside your Claude app, with no app to download.
- **Signed updates.** Hydra-OS checks every page and update against its own key before anything installs.
- **Tips, not a tour.** Each page shows you one thing at the moment it matters.
- **Web searches in general words.** Research searches for topics like yours, never your name, your city or anything you typed.
- **Links open in your browser.** A link on a page opens in your own browser, never beside the chat.
