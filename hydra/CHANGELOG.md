# Hydra-OS

Each version's notes, newest first. Home's What's new shows a version's first three lines once, after an update.

## 1.2.0

- **Research comes with it, free.** It looks into your goals overnight, and what it finds shows on Home.
- **A check-in each day.** Claude asks how one goal is going, and your answer updates it, with Undo.
- **A bit more on Mondays.** Research joins the morning review, which uses a bit more of your Claude plan on Mondays.
- **Wellbeing in the Shop.** Wellbeing is a page you can add now. If you already have it, it stays as it is.

## 1.1.1

- **Goals fill their space.** One to three goals share the band, and the room left over says how to add the next.
- **Quiet spots say what goes there.** Where no page has a question yet, it says one will ask there, answered in a line.
- **Its own Python, on update.** If Hydra-OS ran on the Mac's own Python, this update moves it onto its own.
- **Help on Apple's tools.** If your Mac offers Apple's developer tools, choose Not Now. Hydra-OS doesn't need them.
- **What it set up, said at the end.** Setup's last lines say what's in place, and its log now stays inside ~/Hydra-OS.

## 1.1.0

- **Only the Claude app.** Hydra-OS brings its own Python, checked before it's used. No Apple developer tools.
- **A walkthrough to start.** Setup ends with a short tour in the chat, then asks what you're working toward.
- **On from the start.** The helpers and a short morning review start with setup. Turn either off in Help.
- **Buttons do it themselves.** Update, Repair, Remove and the helpers' switch run right on the page.
- **Health data, a step at a time.** Claude walks you through your iPhone's export, or logs your workouts.

## 1.0.2

- **Buttons instead of phrases.** Set up, Update, Repair and Remove are buttons now, and Claude takes it from there.
- **It mends itself.** Each chat clears what an older Hydra-OS left, and puts the helpers right after a Mac update.
- **What's new first.** Before an update, Claude tells you what it brings, then asks you.

## 1.0.1

- **Adding a page, explained.** Help now says how to add a page from the Shop, and how to set it up after.
- **The Shop at once.** On a new Mac, Home's Shop shows its pages as soon as setup ends.
- **Setup in one step.** Claude asks you once, then sets Hydra-OS up with a single command.
- **Tidier setup.** Setup clears out what an older Hydra-OS left behind, by itself.
- **Repair from Help.** Help's Repair button now reaches Claude, who finds what's wrong and fixes it after your yes.

## 1.0.0

- **Set up in your own chat.** Hydra-OS installs and sets itself up inside your Claude app, with no app to download.
- **Signed updates.** Hydra-OS checks every page and update against its own key before anything installs.
- **Tips, not a tour.** Each page shows you one thing at the moment it matters.
- **Web searches in general words.** Research searches for topics like yours, never your name, your city or anything you typed.
- **Links open in your browser.** A link on a page opens in your own browser, never beside the chat.
