# Wellbeing

Your sleep, bedtime, resting heart and workouts, each against your own usual, from your iPhone's Health export or a
file of days you keep. A free page of Hydra-OS.

## What it reads

- **Your Health export,** which you drop on the page: sleep, resting heart rate and workouts, and nothing else. It never
  reads workout routes, locations or clinical records.
- **Or a file of days,** a spreadsheet saved as .csv, one row a day.
- **No iPhone?** Tell Claude about a workout, and Wellbeing counts it toward your goals and your week.

Your files stay on your Mac, in your Hydra-OS folder. Wellbeing never signs in anywhere and never sends anything.

## What it never does

It never gives a diagnosis, names a medicine, a supplement or a dose, or sets a diet plan or a calorie target. Anything
that looks like a health problem reads "worth asking a doctor".

## Your morning review

Each morning, Claude reads Wellbeing's figures and writes its lines. Each line is checked before it's kept, and holds
only figures Wellbeing worked out. You can turn the morning review off in Help.

## Removing it

Choose Remove Wellbeing… at the foot of its page. It asks first, and your records go to the Trash, so you can take them back.
