# Wellbeing: what changed

## 1.1.1

- A sleep goal set in hours gets one button on Goals' page to count it in nights, with Undo.
- Remove Wellbeing… at the foot of its page takes it out, asking first, and its records go to the Trash.

## 1.1.0

- No iPhone? Tell Claude about a workout, and Wellbeing counts it toward your goals and your week, with no spreadsheet.
- The page shows the export's steps in Apple's own words, and marks the one Claude is walking you through.
- When something needs fixing, Wellbeing's check points to Repair in Help, and never asks you to type anything.

## 1.0.0

The first version of Wellbeing in Hydra-OS: your sleep, bedtime, resting heart and workouts, each against your own usual, from your iPhone's Health export or a file of days, with Food and drink against your sleep.
