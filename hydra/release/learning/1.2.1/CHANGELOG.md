# Research: what changed

## 1.2.1

- Each Monday scouts three of your projects, two searches each, the ones scouted longest ago first. Every project gets its turn, and the Monday's use of your Claude plan stays about the same however many goals you have.

## 1.2.0

- Research comes with Hydra-OS, free, beside Home and Goals. There's nothing to buy or set up: it scouts your goals from the next morning review.
- When a scout's searches don't come back, the morning review stops there rather than searching again the same morning; they run again the next morning.

## 1.1.0

- Research asks you nothing. Your goals are scouted by default, each with Don't scout on its row, and anything you tell Claude you're working on is added, said in one line with its Undo.
- Each project's general topic is matched from its own words, on your Mac. When none matches, Claude picks the closest when you talk about it, and you can change it by telling Claude.
- The morning review sees only each project's general topics, never its name or anything you typed.
- Goals you've finished or set aside are no longer scouted.
- With the morning review off, the page says it fills in once the review is back on.

## 1.0.4

- What changed in Claude's tools reads the Claude API's release notes again, at their new address.
- Before your first scout, Research says it runs at the next morning review, which can be any day.

## 1.0.3

- A plain README: what Research does, what leaves your Mac, what it never does, and where its files live.
- Setup and the README say what the Shop says: its Monday review uses some of your Claude plan.

## 1.0.2

- Setting Research up now says what the Shop says: its weekly run uses some of your Claude plan.

## 1.0.1

- Your Monday scout always finishes: Hydra-OS keeps each search's results for Research as they come back, and Claude saves only the finds.

## 1.0.0

The first version of Research sold in Hydra-OS's Shop.

- A weekly scout for what you're working on: what's new, what you're missing, how it could sell, the openings, what to learn, and Claude skills worth using.
- Your projects come from what you tell Claude, the field on the page, and the goals you choose to scout.
- Searches use Claude's own web search in the morning review, in general words from a fixed list of topics. Never your name, your city or anything you typed.
- Public release notes for Claude's tools, read each morning.
- Your own notes, dropped on the page, stay on your Mac.
